# 回车 CarriageReturn · 素材生图提示词

> 用途：§2.7 素材分层与机器坐标系的生图批。先做 1920s 直立式（默认机），其余三台换变量表里的机型变量。
> 铁律：所有提示词里绝不出现品牌名、型号名、logo、Hanx；一律写 fictional era typewriter。
> 生成后由用户处理：抠图去底、裁带、量锚点，再交桌面端按机器框落位；生图本身不进仓，进仓的是处理后的透明层 + 锚点表。

## 通用禁令（每条提示词都自带，不用另加）

- fictional design only, no brand name, no model name, no logo, no text on the machine body
- straight-on camera, flat front view, no perspective distortion, no tilted angle
- even studio lighting, no harsh shadows, no background clutter, no people, no hands, no other objects
- photorealistic, high detail, sharp edges, no blur, no watermark

## 1920s 直立式（默认机，先做这一台）

### 1. 滚筒纹理层（字车拆层①，可横向连续）

```
A long horizontal typewriter platen roller, fictional 1920s upright typewriter, smooth cream paper surface wrapped around the roller with subtle paper grain and no repeating pattern, no metal bands across the roller, no knobs, no paper guide, no lever, no carriage frame, straight front view, roller filling the full width of the frame and extending beyond the left and right edges, roller thickness about one quarter of the frame height, centered vertically, solid pure white background above and below the roller, photorealistic, even studio lighting, no text, no brand name, no logo, wide aspect ratio
```

- 修正记录（2026-11-04 用户实测）：初版提示词写了 `dark aged metal bands at regular intervals`，模型照字面出了 8 道等距黑环——等距黑环是错的：原机滚筒是光滑奶油面、只有两端金属，没有通体黑环；等距图案平移时会产生周期混叠（移一个周期看起来没动），还会抢纸面文字的视线。此版已删黑环、改光滑面 + 细腻纸纹 + 两端出画；旧图若要抢救，只能把 8 道黑环全部 inpaint 成奶油面，不如重生成一张干净。

- 目标尺寸：越宽越好（21:9 优先，退而求其次 16:9 再裁中间带）；处理时横向平铺/延展，宽 = 机器框宽 + 2 × 最大行程。
- 处理：裁出滚筒带，去白底上下，只留滚筒本体；验连续性——左右拼接处纹理不断。

### 2. 旋钮 sprite（字车拆层②，左右各一张，同一提示词出两张或一张复用）

```
A single round typewriter platen knob, fictional 1920s upright typewriter, black knurled rubber edge, dark aged metal center hub, side view, one knob only, centered, large in frame, solid pure white background, photorealistic, even studio lighting, no text, no brand name, no logo, square aspect ratio
```

- 处理：抠图去白底存透明 PNG，锚点 = 旋钮中心；左右旋钮同一张复用、镜像即可，不必各生成一张。

### 3. 中央金属件 sprite（字车拆层③，机器件，不是光标）

```
A single typewriter paper guide, fictional 1920s upright typewriter, triangular dark aged metal plate with a curved top and a small central window, front view, one piece only, centered, solid pure white background, photorealistic, even studio lighting, no text, no brand name, no logo, square aspect ratio
```

- 处理：抠图去白底存透明 PNG，锚点 = 底部中心；它随字车层整体平移，绝不随光标走。

### 4. 压纸卷层（字车拆层④，可选，若滚筒层已含压纸卷则跳过）

```
A typewriter platen cover and paper bail, fictional 1920s upright typewriter, thin dark aged metal bar with two small rollers, horizontal, front view, one piece only, centered, solid pure white background, photorealistic, even studio lighting, no text, no brand name, no logo, wide aspect ratio
```

### 5. 键区母版（主屏照片键区，空白帽，叠字由代码画）

```
Top-down view of a fictional 1920s upright typewriter keyboard area only, filling the entire frame edge to edge, black metal frame, 4 rows of round cream keycaps, first row 11 caps, second row 10 caps, third row 11 caps, fourth row 11 caps, plus a long black space bar below the fourth row, all keycaps blank with no letters, no numbers, no symbols, uniform cap size except the space bar, straight-on camera, no perspective, no paper, no carriage, no other parts, photorealistic, even studio lighting, no text, no brand name, no logo, square aspect ratio
```

- 目标尺寸：2048×2048。
- 关键：帽子必须空白——照片上有字母的话，代码叠字会双重叠影；帽数若与 11/10/11/11 对不上，不要硬凑，量出实际帽数、重做 keymap。
- 处理：按母版裁手机带/折叠带（见 §2.3），量每帽中心进 keymap。

### 6. 字杆层（竖直杆，支点在下、杆尖在上）

```
A single typewriter typebar arm, fictional 1920s upright typewriter, long thin dark aged metal arm, vertical, with a small type slug at the top end and a pivot hole at the bottom end, front view, one arm only, centered, solid pure white background, photorealistic, even studio lighting, no text, no brand name, no logo, tall vertical aspect ratio
```

- 处理：抠图去白底存透明 PNG，锚点 = 支点（底部中心）；支点比例、杆长比例按机器框登记，不许用母版像素。

### 7. 拨杆层（换行拨杆）

```
A typewriter carriage return lever, fictional 1920s upright typewriter, long curved dark aged metal lever with a black round handle at the end, side view, one lever only, centered, solid pure white background, photorealistic, even studio lighting, no text, no brand name, no logo, tall vertical aspect ratio
```

### 8. 整机平视参考图（只用于登记锚点，不进主屏）

```
Flat front view of a complete fictional 1920s upright typewriter, cream paper sheet above, horizontal carriage roller in the middle, keyboard below, all in one frame, centered, straight-on camera, no perspective, dark brown body color #312b24, cream keycaps color #dbccb3, cream paper color #eee4ce, solid dark background, no other objects, no text, no brand name, no logo, photorealistic, even studio lighting, landscape aspect ratio
```

- 用途：量打击点、旋钮中心、纸左右缘、纸下缘搭接量在机器框里的位置；主屏不用这张整机图（hero 只用于解锁庆祝背景，见 §8.1）。

## 其余三台变量表（提示词骨架不变，只换下面几行）

| 机型 | 机身/帽子 | 键区行数（帽数） | 备注 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1930s 便携式 | 黑色机身、深色圆帽 | 11 / 11 / 12 / 11 / 11，另加空格条两侧各一枚大圆帽 | 第 5 排目前 keymap 全留空，生图时第 5 排帽子空白即可，用途待定 |
| 1950s 流线型 | 浅色机身、奶油圆帽 | 12 / 13 / 12 / 12 | 帽子偏圆润、机身线条流线 |
| 1960s 电动式 | 深灰机身、浅色方帽 | 14 / 13 / 13 / 12 | 帽子方形、行首尾宽帽保留，主帽统一 |

- 换法示例（1930s 键区）：把提示词 5 里的 `fictional 1920s upright typewriter` 换成 `fictional 1930s portable typewriter`，`round cream keycaps` 换成 `round dark keycaps`，行数换成上表，机身颜色描述换成黑色；其余禁令、空白帽、顶视、方形尺寸一律不变。
- 字杆、旋钮、中央件、滚筒四层，每台各出一套，风格跟本机走，不许跨机混用。

## 生成后交给桌面端的东西（缺一不可）

1. 处理后的透明层 PNG（滚筒纹理、旋钮、中央件、压纸卷、字杆、拨杆）+ 键区母版 JPG + 整机平视参考图。
2. 锚点表：打击点、左右旋钮中心、纸左右缘、纸下缘搭接量、每帽中心与帽直径，全部按机器框（宽 1000 单位）登记，不是像素、不是百分比混用。
3. 验图记录：每层 alpha bbox、帽数实测（与上表对不上就写实际数）、颜色是否对上本机色板。
4. 测试截图 `_w*.jpeg` 之类不进仓根目录（见 §2.7），分析完即移出。
